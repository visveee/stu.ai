# stu.ai

all-in-one recruiting assistant for students going after tech, finance, and consulting roles

## The problem

recruiting for internships/full-time is scattered across dozens of company career pages, job boards, and diversity program sites. applications open and close quickly (often within the span of a week), deadlines are easy to miss, and every site is organized differently. students end up juggling spreadsheets, bookmarks, and email alerts just to keep track of what's open.

## What it will do

track openings across tech, finance, and consulting in one place, including internships/diversity programs
alert students as soon as an application opens, not days later
show deadlines, requirements, and eligibility in a single clear format
keep personal pipeline of what you've applied to, what's next, and what's due
